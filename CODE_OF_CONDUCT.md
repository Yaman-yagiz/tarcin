# Code of conduct

tarcin is made by volunteers for people who live in the terminal. We
want everyone who uses it, reports a bug or sends a change to be
treated with respect.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, also when you disagree. Criticise ideas and
  code, never people.
- Assume good faith. Many contributors write in a language that is not
  their first.
- Welcome newcomers, and help them find their way.
- Accept a decision once it is made, and the reasons recorded with it;
  argue for a change with evidence, in a new issue.
- Keep conversations on topic.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning remarks about anyone's
  identity, background or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as their phone number
  or messages, without their consent.
- Pressing for features tarcin will not have, such as bulk messaging,
  scraping or other automation, after they were declined.

## Where it applies

In the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space
the project runs, and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, through the contact on the
GitHub profile of [@Yaman-yagiz](https://github.com/Yaman-yagiz). Reports
are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads,
or ban someone from the project, temporarily or for good, and says why.
