# tarcin 0.1.0 (alpha)

The first release that connects to WhatsApp. tarcin links to your
account as a companion device and lets you read and write text chats in
the terminal. It is an **alpha**: use it with a test account, and expect
rough edges.

> **Read this first.** tarcin is an unofficial client. WhatsApp's terms do
> not allow third-party clients, and an account that uses one can be
> restricted or banned. tarcin keeps your messages and the device's keys
> unencrypted on disk, so use full-disk encryption. tarcin is not
> affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

## What works

- **Linking** by QR code, by a code for your phone number, or by the
  passkey hand-off some accounts require. tarcin shows the account risk
  and where your data is kept before it connects at all.
- **Your history**: the chats and messages your phone sends after linking
  are stored as they arrive, and an import cut short by quitting resumes
  at the next start.
- **Reading**: text, captions and quoted replies (`gq` jumps to the
  quoted message); a placeholder for every other kind (images, voice
  notes, documents, polls…); edits and deletions; reactions; mentions,
  by name, with mentions of you marked; names from your contacts; pinned, muted and archived chats; unread counts that
  agree with your phone. Scrolling past the oldest stored message asks
  your phone for older ones.
- **Media**: images, videos, voice notes, documents and stickers open in
  your programs (`o`, `xdg-open` or openers per MIME type) and save to
  your downloads (`s`), downloaded on demand within a size limit into a
  private, size-capped cache. `:attach PATH` sends a file, with Tab to
  complete the path.
- **Writing**: text messages and replies (`R`), with mentions completed
  from the group's members (`@`, Tab), sent in the order you type them,
  with delivery and read ticks; reactions (`r`); editing
  your messages (`e`, for 20 minutes) and deleting them for everyone
  (`dd`); forwarding (`f`), media without uploading it again. A failed
  message stays in the chat until you retry it with ctrl+r. `y` copies a
  message's text and `v` shows it in `$PAGER`.
- **Groups**: `:members` lists a group's members, admins first; an
  opened group's member list is refreshed from the server when it is
  more than a day old.
- **Read receipts, typing and online status**, each with a setting in the
  config (`privacy.read_receipts`, `typing`, `presence`). tarcin is
  online only while you use it, not whenever it is running. The chat
  header shows when someone types, and whether a direct chat's peer is
  online.
- **Staying connected**: the status line says what the connection is
  doing, tarcin reconnects within seconds when the network returns, and
  it explains when WhatsApp ends the session (logged out, banned,
  replaced or outdated).
- **Disappearing messages** follow the chat's timer, sent and deleted as
  on the phone; view-once media points you to the phone.
- **Chat settings**: `:mute`, `:pin` and `:archive` and their opposites,
  shared with your phone; `]u` and `[u` jump between unread chats.
- **Search**: `:find` searches the messages of all chats and `:findin` the
  open one, Turkish-aware; Enter opens a result where it was written.
- **Desktop notifications** for new messages while the terminal does not
  have focus, not for muted chats or a backlog; the bell without a
  desktop.
- **A responsive UI** that does not freeze in large, busy groups.
- **Configuration** in `~/.config/tarcin/config.toml`: the privacy
  settings, the theme (`NO_COLOR` aware, and a high-contrast one), media
  programs, notifications, the log level and every key binding.
  `tarcin config print-defaults` prints a commented file.
- **`tarcin backup FILE`**, which saves your messages and settings, also
  while tarcin runs, and **`tarcin logout`**, which unlinks the device
  and can delete your local data.
- **Man pages** (`man tarcin`, `man 5 tarcin`), shell completions for
  bash, zsh and fish, a desktop file, and a [user guide](../user/README.md).
- **Private by default**: no telemetry; a log without message content,
  names or phone numbers; private file modes.

## Not yet

- Calls, statuses and payments, which tarcin will not have.
- Polls, stickers, locations and contact cards show as placeholders;
  images open in your viewer rather than inline.

## Install

From the AUR, once the packages are published:

```sh
yay -S tarcin
```

Or from source on Arch Linux:

```sh
sudo pacman -S --needed go base-devel git scdoc
git clone https://github.com/Yaman-yagiz/tarcin
cd tarcin
git checkout v0.1.0
make
sudo make install PREFIX=/usr
```

The build needs Go 1.26 or newer and a C compiler (SQLite is linked
through cgo). See the [install guide](../user/install.md).

## Where your data is

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Messages and device keys | `~/.local/share/tarcin/profiles/default/` |
| Log | `~/.local/state/tarcin/tarcin.log` |
| Configuration | `~/.config/tarcin/config.toml` |

`tarcin backup FILE` saves your messages and settings without the
device's keys. Back up the keys, or `~/.local/share/tarcin`, only to
places as private as your disk: anyone with a copy can read your
messages and act as this device. See [data and privacy](../user/data.md).

## Reporting problems

Report problems as GitHub issues, with the time they happened
and the log lines around it; check the lines before you post them.
Report security problems privately, as [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md)
describes, never in a public issue.

The full list of changes is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
